Hey, I'm Abhi. This is AbhiRoams, a place for me to write about the places I go, the food I eat, and the things worth paying attention to when you're somewhere new. It's part travel journal, part restaurant recommendation list, part excuse to take more trips.

The content will be pretty varied. Sometimes it'll be a deep dive on a city I spent a week in. Sometimes it'll be a single restaurant that made the whole trip worth it. Occasionally a hike or a road trip or something that just seemed like a good idea at the time. No strict format, no niche — just what I found interesting.

If you're here, welcome. More posts are coming. In the meantime, you know where to find me.
